## 2. What they look for in founders

All of the following are from Bhagia's Inc42 interview: [P] https://inc42.com/features/kushal-baghia-all-in-capital-founder-market-fit-framework-pre-seed-investing
- **"Why now" comes first.** Before looking at founders, they ask what has changed in the world, often in technology, that makes the idea possible now.
- **Founder-market fit.** What the founders know and can or cannot do. The founder must hold the key skills the opportunity needs.
- **Earned secrets.** The team should name the key risk and the insights they gained from past experience.
- **Proof of building.** First-time founders need evidence they can build, for example having shipped a consumer tech product or run products, services or transactions at scale.
- **"Dil Chahta Hai" teams.** Close friends, or co-founders with long shared work history and complementary skills.
- **Conviction and stamina.** Founders should hold a thesis, love the problem, and be ready to commit for 5–6 years.
- **Contra-bets only.** Bhagia says there is no safe bet at pre-seed, so the fund only makes contrarian bets. Giva is cited as an example.

From the site and press:
- The firm's name reflects backing founders who go "all in". Source: [S] https://allincapital.vc ; [P] https://inc42.com/buzz/all-in-capital-launches-inr-200-cr-fund-ii
- Golden Ticket coverage describes backing "non-obvious founders" early, with emphasis on speed and founder alignment. Source: [P] https://entrackr.com/snippets/all-in-capital-launches-golden-ticket-program-for-early-stage-ai-saas-founders-11074761
- Aditya Singh says pre-seed is where the real risk and the real opportunity sit. Source: [P] https://inc42.com/buzz/all-in-capital-launches-inr-200-cr-fund-ii

Support offered: funding, a 100+ founder community, VC introductions, narrative help, and WhatsApp access to the partners ("2 AM friends"). Source: [S] https://allincapital.vc

### Stated anti-thesis (what they avoid)
- **Safe or obvious bets.** Source: [P] https://inc42.com/features/kushal-baghia-all-in-capital-founder-market-fit-framework-pre-seed-investing
- **Later-stage companies with revenue and profits.** They write small cheques and would be competing with large funds. Same source.
- **Crypto, capital-heavy quick commerce, or any explicit excluded-category list: not found publicly** (see the last section). QuestBook (Web3 learning) sits in the older portfolio grid, and Plazza (instant medicine delivery) appears on an aggregator list. So a hard "no Web3" or "no instant delivery" rule cannot be sourced.

## 4. Application process

All of the following are from [S] https://allincapital.vc/apply_now
- **Three routes in:**
  1. Get one of the listed founder-angel venture partners to invest (even about $5K); All In will most likely invest alongside them.
  2. An introduction from a portfolio founder.
  3. A direct application form.
- **Form:** https://superdm.com/kushal. It has 5 sections and takes about 10 minutes, covering team, problem and market. Founders are told to do it in one sitting and give detail. A question list is linked as a Google Doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/13pEWGPk9wPLhXhzofUeK-jzoDh3dVYHyyoJAK-lYNqw/edit. That doc's body did not load without sign-in, so the exact questions were not captured.
- **Response time:** They reply within 3 days of submission. After a call, they give a yes or no within 10 days of submission.
- **Speed proof point:** The PierSight founder story says first call to term sheet took 11 days. Source: [S] https://allincapital.vc/portfolio
